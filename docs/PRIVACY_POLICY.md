# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 2026

## 1. Introduction

Matjenin ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered vector search platform and related services (the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Service.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and company information
- **Documents**: Documents you upload for processing are stored securely and used solely to provide our vector search and RAG services
- **Chat Data**: Conversations you have with our AI are processed to provide responses but are not used to train our models
- **Payment Information**: If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment processing is handled by third-party providers (we do not store credit card information)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: Information about how you use the Service, including features accessed, time spent, and actions taken
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, browser type, and unique device identifiers
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, pages viewed, and other system activity

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- We may receive information from third-party integrations you authorize (e.g., authentication providers)

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Provide Services | Process your documents, generate embeddings, and power AI responses |
| Account Management | Create and maintain your account, verify your identity |
| Communication | Send service updates, respond to inquiries, provide support |
| Improvement | Analyze usage patterns to improve our Service |
| Security | Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats |
| Legal Compliance | Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms |

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage

- **Database**: Your data is stored in secure SQL Server databases with tenant isolation
- **Vector Storage**: Document embeddings are stored in Weaviate vector database
- **Backups**: Regular automated backups are performed

### 4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access control (RBAC) and multi-tenant isolation
- **Authentication**: Secure session management and API key authentication
- **Monitoring**: Continuous security monitoring and audit logging
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments performed regularly

### 4.3 Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Account Data | While account is active, plus 30 days after deletion |
| Documents | Until you delete them or your account |
| Chat History | 30 days by default (configurable) |
| Logs | 90 days for security and debugging |
| Backups | 30 days |

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 General Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Access | Request copies of your personal data |
| Rectification | Request correction of inaccurate data |
| Erasure | Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") |
| Restrict Processing | Request restriction of processing |
| Data Portability | Request transfer of your data to another service |
| Object | Object to certain processing activities |

### 6.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect and how it's used
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Opt-out of the sale of your personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

### 6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:

1. **Account Settings**: Log in to your account and use the dashboard
2. **Contact Us**: Email support@matjenin.space with your request
3. **Data Export**: Use the export feature in your dashboard

We will respond to your request within 30 days.
